01 Applied Machine Learning · IEEE Publication

Explainable AI for
Credit Card Fraud Detection

How can a fraud-detection model be trusted if it cannot be explained?

Evaluated four machine-learning models on credit card fraud detection, then applied SHAP to translate model behaviour into interpretable insight for business and technical stakeholders. The work became a peer-reviewed IEEE publication, presented at InCIT 2023.

First Author & Presenter
Models

02 Computer Vision · Final Year Project

Automatic Grading
Assessment of Cocoa Seeds

Grading cocoa seeds by hand is slow and inconsistent.

My undergraduate final-year project implemented YOLOv5 and YOLOv8 to automate grading through image recognition — carried from user requirements to a working AI prototype with measurable output and practical use-case relevance.

Approach
YOLOv5 · YOLOv8
Scope
Requirements analysis → AI prototype
Context
